# Company and social logos

These identify companies named in Gabriel's existing experience; they do not
imply endorsement. Downloaded 31 October 2026 and served locally.

- `temasek.svg`: Temasek Holdings wordmark, from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:TemasekHoldings_logo.svg).
  [Original SVG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/43/TemasekHoldings_logo.svg).
  Commons identifies Temasek Holdings as the author and marks it PD-textlogo.
- `elefant.svg`: Elefant logo mark from its
  [official homepage](https://elefant.net.ai/),
  [original SVG](https://elefant.net.ai/_nuxt/elefant-header-logo.DKkj3bTR.svg).
  The downloaded asset is unchanged; CSS displays its white mark in black on
  the light bubble background.
- `foremark.png`: Foremark Legal's light-mode favicon from its
  [official homepage](https://foremarklegal.com/),
  [original PNG](https://framerusercontent.com/images/SQN6USStwr9rwwieh6TmUkbb9o.png).
- `mha.png`: Ministry of Home Affairs' crest, used as the favicon on its
  [official homepage](https://www.mha.gov.sg/),
  [original PNG](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/341/7d1afbd6-ffc7-4362-a9ed-f7bf7e205ca0/mha-logo-2x.png).
- `x.svg`: X's logo from the [Simple Icons project](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/x.svg),
  distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
  The SVG is unchanged; CSS inverts it on dark backgrounds.
