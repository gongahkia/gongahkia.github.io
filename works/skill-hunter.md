`Skill Hunter` was my first substantial project *(built for people other than just myself)* that also catapulted me into the wonderful world of browser extension scripting.

More significantly, building and deploying Skill Hunter showed me that more often than not, the products with the highest adoption metrics are the tools that solve a specific problem effectively with minimal installation friction. Not every problem requires a full-blown app.

If reading SSO Legislation or eLitigation Cases feels overwhelming, consider checking it out!
